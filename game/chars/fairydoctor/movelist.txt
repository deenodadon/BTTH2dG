Little Fairy Doctor - move list
========================================

Default keys: A light punch | S heavy punch | Z light kick | X heavy kick
             D special button | C super button | Enter taunt
Throw: Forward or Back + S (close) | Run: Forward,Forward | Hop back: Back,Back | Air recovery: A+S

Move                                   Easy input (classic input)     
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Poison Mist                            D (QCF + P)                    Slow, lingering four-hit toxic cloud
Toxic Palm                             Forward + D (QCB + P)          Stepping poisonous palm
Poison Field                           Down + D (D, D + P)            Area denial: poison pools on the ground for 3 seconds
Woeful Poison Body                     C (1 bar) (QCF, QCF + P)       Invincible toxic mist explosion
Poison Calamity                        Down + C (2 bars) (QCB, QCB + P) Miasma rain over the foe

Yao Lao - move list
========================================

Default keys: A light punch | S heavy punch | Z light kick | X heavy kick
             D special button | C super button | Enter taunt
Throw: Forward or Back + S (close) | Run: Forward,Forward | Hop back: Back,Back | Air recovery: A+S

Move                                   Easy input (classic input)     
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Bone Chilling Flame                    D (QCF + P)                    Freezing white flame projectile
Soul Shock                             Forward + D (QCB + P)          Unblockable soul-wave that bypasses guard
Advanced Alchemy: Healing Pill         Down + D (half bar) (D, D + P) Refines and eats a pill to restore life
Spirit Drift                           Back + D (QCB + K)             Ghostly teleport (behind the foe when close)
Bone Chilling Flame Torrent            C (1 bar) (QCF, QCF + P)       Multi-hit frost beam
Soul Realm Domination                  Down + C (2 bars) (QCB, QCB + P) Unblockable giant soul palm crashes on the foe

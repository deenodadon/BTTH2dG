Yun Yun - move list
========================================

Default keys: A light punch | S heavy punch | Z light kick | X heavy kick
             D special button | C super button | Enter taunt
Throw: Forward or Back + S (close) | Run: Forward,Forward | Hop back: Back,Back | Air recovery: A+S

Move                                   Easy input (classic input)     
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Wind Blade                             D (QCF + P)                    Sword slash that throws a wind crescent
Rising Gale                            Down + D (F, D, DF + P)        Spinning anti-air slash
Wind Step                              Back + D (QCB + K)             Wind Lightning Pavilion teleport
Sword Storm                            C (1 bar) (QCF, QCF + P)       Six slashes ending in a tornado
Thousand Wind Swords                   Down + C (2 bars) (QCB, QCB + P) Wind swords rain down on the foe
